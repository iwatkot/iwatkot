## Hi! I'm Stan 👋

I'm a software engineer who loves maps. I care about what code can do for people: let software carry the repetition and the complexity, and let people keep the ideas.

GIS is the part I enjoy most. Terrain, imagery, OpenStreetMap data, strange coordinate systems, two datasets that insist the same point exists in two different places. Give me those and I'm happy.

### MapToPlay

<a href="https://maptoplay.com"><img src="images/maptoplay-banner-dark.png" width="838" alt="MapToPlay. Real places turned into playable game maps"></a>

I'm the creator and sole developer of [MapToPlay](https://maptoplay.com). It turns real places into playable game maps and mods: terrain, imagery and OpenStreetMap data go into a live editor, and maps come out for Farming Simulator, BeamNG.drive, SnowRunner, RoadCraft, Minecraft, Project Zomboid and more. I build all of it, from the product and UX to the infrastructure and the support inbox. There is no mysterious other department to blame. I checked.
